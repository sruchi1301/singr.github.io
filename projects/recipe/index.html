Materials

  • Paper
  • Pen/Marker

Instructions

  1. Draw a circle.
  2. Draw two triangles on the top of the circle - not too far, but not too close
  3. Draw two smaller triangles inside the first ones.
  4. Draw two circles inside the ellipse.
  5. Draw two smaller, filled-in circles inside the ones you just drew, touching the bottom of the bigger circles but remaining inside.
  6. Draw a small upside-down triangle below and in between the “eyes” you just drew, creating the nose.
  7. Draw a lowercase w below that, with curved joints, with the middle part of the w touching the tip of the upside-down triangle.
  8. Draw three lines branching out from either side of your triangle, leaving a small gap between the start of the lines and the middle of the nose.
