Reflection

Write a short description about things you’re noticing about your submissions so far. What is similar and what is different between them? What have you learned so far? What do you want to experiment with next?

My submissions, in terms of design, definitely lean towards more minimalist text and background with simple ornamentation or small details. I also like seeing how to layout and format single pages rather than constantly linking everything to different pages. Figuring out how to organise content into a single page is an interesting design problem.

I also prefer to experiment with digging deeper into the technical limits of HTML/CSS rather than only thinking about the visual design of the website at face value. The inner workings are very interesting to me.

My pages are different in the way I like to think I adapted them for their functions. The visual website, for example, is very simple with just the image because it's the highlight, while I took more liberties with the recipe and experimented with list styles and symbols because the content was simple.

I've learnt a lot more about applying HTML and CSS to solve specific problems, because my previous experience with them was only supplementing Python, so learning them by themselves with the visual being the focus has been really educational and also more fun than I expected, because I never really understood how much you could do with HTML and CSS. I think moving forward I'm really excited to experiment with JavaScript and connecting all three together to make fully functional pages.

Something I think I will also spend some more time studying is flexbox and also CSS grids, because I've been struggling with applying those to my code. Also, CSS positioning is evading me.
