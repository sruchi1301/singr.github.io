We're now going a little further from Grove St.

I saw the Colgate Clock, which is apparently an important artifact. It's a lot more intricate when you step up close, and my curiosity was definitely piqued. I learned that:

  1. It was built in 1924, for Colgate's centennial
  2. It used to be on top of the Colgate plant at 85-99 Hudson Street in Jersey City, New Jersey
  3. The clock is a mandatory reporting point for flights below Class B airspace in the Hudson River VFR corridor
